The Irish Breakout & Martial Proclamation

On the night of March 4, 1804, over 200 United Irishmen political exiles seized weapons and set fire to the superintendent’s hut at Castle Hill, intending to link up with the Hawkesbury rebels. Governor King suspended civil magistrates and declared martial law across Parramatta, Ryde, and the Hawkesbury within six hours.

Source: HRNSW Vol. V, pp. 345–349; Governor King's Martial Proclamation (5 March 1804).
